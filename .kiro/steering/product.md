# Product Overview

HUNKCentral is a digital workforce management system that replaces paper logs and Excel spreadsheets for College Hunks Hauling Junk & Moving. The system streamlines daily operations by enabling captains to submit work logs, managers to review and approve them, sales staff to track commissions, and administrators to generate payroll reports.

## Core Business Functions

- **Captain Daily Logs**: Multi-section forms for recording Junk jobs, Move jobs, and Other hours with real-time labor cost calculations
- **Manager Review**: Workflow for reviewing, editing, and approving submitted logs with bulk operations
- **Commission Tracking**: Sales staff can enter job bookings that automatically match to completed work for commission calculation
- **Payroll Reports**: Comprehensive compensation reports supporting mixed pay models (hourly, salary, commission, bonuses)
- **User Management**: Role-based system with detailed compensation settings for each employee

## Key Business Rules

- **Labor Bonus Goals**: Junk operations target 14% labor cost, Move operations target 24%
- **Rate Application**: Wingman rates by default, Captain rates for captains and co-captains
- **Commission Matching**: Automatic matching of sales bookings to completed jobs on log approval
- **Mixed Compensation**: Employees can have any combination of hourly, salary, commission, and bonus pay
- **Audit Trail**: Complete tracking of all log creation, editing, and approval activities

## Success Metrics

- Captain log submission in under 2 minutes
- Manager review in under 30 seconds per log
- Page load times under 1 second
- Mobile-first design for field workers
